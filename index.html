Tender pipeline

Revenue starts at expected award + mobilisation months, weighted by win probability × scenario factor. Be honest with probabilities — an EOI is not a tender.

Global assumptions

Scenario factors

Multiply renewal and win probabilities (capped at 100%); uptime shift applies to bus contracts in percentage points.

Method

Bus monthly revenue = buses × km/bus/day × days in month × uptime × ₹/km × escalation × (1 − penalty %). Cab/Manpower = units × monthly rate × escalation. Escalation compounds monthly from the "rates as of" month (proxy for price-variation clauses: fuel, minimum wages). EBITDA = revenue × segment margin. Working capital = revenue ÷ 365 × receivable days — in B2G this is usually what breaks cash flow, not margin.
